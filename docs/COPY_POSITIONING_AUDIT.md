# Copy & Positioning Audit (2026-09-10)

Source: copy audit provided by Feby, cross-referenced against her respond.io-tailored CV. Goal: keep the site warm, personal, and visually distinctive, while making the copy credible, recruiter-friendly, and accurate to her actual seniority, AI experience, and target roles (not fintech-only).

This document is the **content specification** — see `TASKS.md`'s "Phase 1.2 — Copy & Positioning Overhaul" for the execution checklist derived from it, ordered into shippable batches.

## Final positioning

Core positioning, used across navbar, footer, hero, and metadata:

**Product Manager · Consumer & Enterprise Products · Applied AI**

Longer positioning, used in the hero body or About section:

> I build customer-facing and enterprise product experiences across onboarding, lifecycle engagement, open banking APIs, cash management systems, and applied AI workflows.

## Copy principles

- Use **Product Manager**, not **Senior Product Manager** or **Senior PM** — her last official title at Hijra was Product Manager.
- Do not position Feby as a pure **AI PM**. Position her as a **Product Manager with applied AI shipping experience**.
- Avoid overusing "fintech" in the main slogan — the portfolio should support applications to SaaS, AI, customer lifecycle, automation, and non-fintech PM roles. Fintech evidence stays in case studies and experience; the top-level message stays broader (customer lifecycle, automation, 0→1 products, enterprise products, applied AI).
- Use grounded verbs: co-led, helped ship, built, launched with selected users, improved, reduced, migrated, designed, evaluated.
- Avoid hyperbolic phrases — "frontier products", "flawless", "high-scale impact", "zero margin for error", "rock-solid", "wheelhouse", "delight" — unless backed by specific evidence.
- Keep confidential case studies locked and clearly marked with a lock icon.
- Add public-facing sections that show learning, independent building, and practical AI usage (Iqro Land, writing/LinkedIn posts).

## Section-level acceptance criteria

- No visible copy says "Senior Product Manager" or "Senior PM".
- Main positioning says "Product Manager · Consumer & Enterprise Products · Applied AI".
- Hero does not over-index on fintech.
- AI copy uses "applied AI", "AI-enabled", "NLP banking assistant", "chatbot automation", or "AI-assisted prototyping" — not a pure "AI PM" framing.
- Confidential case-study CTAs include 🔐.
- MISHA is described as "limited-release" or "selected-user rollout".
- H2H/API and CBS experience are represented through enterprise/platform language.
- Iqro Land section exists with clearly-labeled screenshot placeholders (confirmed approach for now).
- Learning in Public section exists with the 3 real LinkedIn post links (Arabic letter tracing, local-first vs. cloud, Git study notes).
- Footer LinkedIn link is not the generic `https://linkedin.com`.
- Contact copy supports Product Manager roles, customer lifecycle, automation, applied AI, and writing/speaking collaboration.

## Tone

Professional but warm; specific but not boastful; credible for recruiters; broad enough for non-fintech PM roles; strong for respond.io-style roles (customer lifecycle, messaging, automation, AI-enabled workflows). Avoid sounding like a VC landing page — the strongest version of this site is grounded, specific, and shows that Feby learns fast and ships practical product work.
